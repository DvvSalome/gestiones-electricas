Executive Summary: Is low-cost residential wind energy viable? Small domestic wind turbines complement self-generation solar production in rural or isolated areas without grid access. An integrated engineering design and the combination of renewable technologies guarantee clean, constant energy, eliminating dependency on fossil fuels.

Indian startup Avant Garde Innovations has designed and developed a low cost wind turbine which can generate 3-5 kWh of electricity per day. A small wind turbine Suitable for residential, commercial, agricultural areas, which will be launched in this year. Many areas of India are still in the dark, without access to the commercial electrical grid. For a large section of the Indian population, electricity today remains a distant dream. But two brothers want to make this energy crisis in India a thing of the past. They have developed a new takeaway solution renewable energy to Indian homes, eliminating energy poverty. Avant Garde Innovations, the startup founded by brothers Arun and Anoop George from Kerala, has developed a low-cost wind turbine that can generate enough electricity to power an entire house for a lifetime. About the size of a ceiling fan, this turbine can generate up to 5 kWh per day, priced at US$750.
