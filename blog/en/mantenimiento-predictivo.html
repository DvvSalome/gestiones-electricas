🔍 The Difference Between "Checking" and "Predicting"

Traditional preventive maintenance consists of cleaning, tightening bolts, and performing calendar-based routines. Although necessary, it is blind. It cannot tell you if the insulation of an underground cable is degrading or if your transformer core is overheating.

Predictive Maintenance, on the other hand, applies diagnostic technology and data analysis to evaluate the actual "health" of your equipment while operating under full load. We don't guess; we measure.

🛠️ Technologies that Save Infrastructure

To guarantee the operational continuity of our clients, Gestiones Eléctricas applies specialized diagnostic tests:

  • Infrared Thermography: Electrical faults generate heat before generating fire. We use high-precision thermographic cameras to scan panels, breakers, and transformers. This allows us to detect loose connections, phase imbalances, or overloads invisible to the human eye, stopping a potential arc flash or fire.
  • VLF (Very Low Frequency) Testing: Underground medium-voltage cables are the arteries of your industry. A VLF test under IEEE standards subjects the cable to controlled stress to identify micro-cracks in the insulation caused by moisture or aging, preventing the cable from exploding underground and paralyzing your operation.
  • Physical-Chemical Oil Analysis: The oil of a transformer is like blood in the human body. By extracting a sample and analyzing it in a lab, we evaluate dielectric breakdown voltage, moisture levels, and dissolved gases. This tells us if the internal insulating paper is degrading, allowing us to perform thermal treatment before the transformer collapses.

💰 The ROI of Predictive Engineering

Many administrators view specialized tests as an additional expense. However, the numbers dictate a different reality. Implementing a structured predictive program achieves three immediate financial goals:

  1. Maximizes asset lifespan: A well-monitored transformer can easily exceed 20 years of continuous service.
  2. Eliminates emergency surcharges: Buying an industrial replacement on a Sunday at 3:00 a.m. costs up to three times more than planning its replacement a month in advance.
  3. Guarantees safety: Complies with industrial safety and RETIE standard standards, protecting your collaborators' lives and shielding the company legally.
