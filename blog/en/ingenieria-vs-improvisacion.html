Engineering vs. Improvisation: Why Choose Certified Engineers?

5 MIN READ · Published: April 01, 2026
GE

Engineering Department

Gestiones Eléctricas SAS

Executive Summary: Why hire certified engineers for electrical projects? Improvisation with unlicensed personnel violates the law and puts lives and infrastructure at risk. Only engineers with active professional registration (CONTE/COPNIA) can sign designs and guarantee installations comply with RETIE standard and NTC 2050 electrical code, an essential requirement for insurance approvals.

By: Engineering Department – Gestiones Eléctricas SAS

In the energy sector, there is a huge difference between "getting the equipment to turn on" and "making the installation safe, efficient, and compliant with codes."

As engineers, we often arrive at projects where an empirical electrician or handyman did the installation. The result is usually the same: overheated conductors, poorly sized protection, and, most seriously, a latent risk to human life and infrastructure.

On the Caribbean Coast, where environmental conditions (salinity, humidity, and temperature) are aggressive, electricity does not forgive improvisation. Here, we explain why hiring an expert firm and certified CONTE technicians is not an expense, but the only way to shield your investment.

⚖️ 1. Compliance (RETIE): What you can't see counts too

In Colombia, the Technical Regulation of Electrical Installations (RETIE) is not a suggestion; it is the Law.

An informal installer can connect a wire, but only a Registered Professional Electrical Engineer has the legal and technical authority to:

  • Design and sign blueprints to be approved by the Grid Operator (Air-e/Afinia).
  • Guarantee that the materials used have product certifications.
  • Ensure that, in the event of an audit or an accident, your company is legally protected.

The risk: An installation that does not comply with RETIE can result in power disconnection or insurers refusing to pay in case of fire.

🧠 2. Engineering Calculations: Efficiency costs less

Empiricism works by guesswork. Engineering works by calculation.

When we address a project at Gestiones Eléctricas SAS (whether a substation, medium voltage line, or solar installation), we don't guess. We perform:

  • Load Charts: To avoid oversizing (overspending) or undersizing (fire hazard) the conductors.
  • Short-Circuit Analysis and Protection Coordination: So that if a fault occurs, only the correct breaker trips, and your entire plant doesn't go dark.
  • Power Quality Studies: To detect harmonics or low power factor that inflates your monthly bill.

🛡️ 3. CONTE Technicians: Expert and Safe Hands

A good design is not enough; execution must be flawless. Our operational staff holds CONTE (National Council of Electrical Technicians) professional registration.

What does this guarantee?

  1. Technical Knowledge: They know how to interpret single-line diagrams and respect color codes and safety standards.
  2. Industrial Safety: They work under the 5 Golden Rules, use dielectric PPE, and hold valid heights certifications.
  3. Accountability: An established firm responds. An informal worker, in the event of major damage, simply stops answering the phone.

🔬 4. Diagnostic Technology: We Measure, We Don't Guess

Modern engineering relies on data. Unlike a common electrician who only uses a basic multimeter, at Gestiones Eléctricas SAS we invest in state-of-the-art technology for precise diagnostics:

  • Thermographic Cameras: To see hot spots invisible to the human eye before they cause downtime.
  • VLF (Very Low Frequency) Equipment: To test insulation integrity in medium voltage cables without damaging them.
  • Digital Ground Testers: To certify that your grounding grid actually protects against atmospheric discharges.
  • Owned Bucket Truck: For safe maneuvers at heights and aerial grids.
